# Demo: 7 slides (or a 30-second video)

The rules allow a deck of at most seven slides or a video of at most 30 seconds. English only. Host it at a public link.
A built deck is in `demo-deck.pptx` / `demo-deck.pdf`; this file is its script.

## Slides

1. **Title.** The Last Trend Bender. "Environmental fingerprints of war, seen from space." Team **[name]**: **[members]**. Challenge: Be an Earth System Trend Detective.
2. **The question.** One variable can rise in one place and fall in another. Which changes are real, and which are noise? We tested it where the answer matters: Ukraine, 2015–2022.
3. **The tool.** Screenshot: choose a place and a period. Seven places in Ukraine, two comparison cities, 25 NASA variables in four groups: Air, Fire, Water, Earth.
4. **The cards.** Screenshot: what is changing, where, how much per year, and is it significant. Hollow points are months the satellite barely saw.
5. **What we found.** NO₂ in March–August 2022 against 2015–2019: Kharkiv −29%, Kyiv −28%, Kraków −11%, Prague −7.5%. A shock in the cities under attack. But across 368 tests, the only significant long-term trend is Prague, and it began before the war.
6. **Why trust it.** NASA OMI, VIIRS and POWER data. Seasons removed, gaps flagged, 368 tests corrected for luck. "Too little data" is shown as a result. Every decision is logged in the repository.
7. **Next.** Longer records, more regions, a test for sudden changes. The same pipeline works for any conflict or disaster. Link to the repository.
